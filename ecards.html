Sodium

Sodium is a mineral that helps maintain fluid balance, nerve function, and muscle contractions.

Too much sodium can lead to high blood pressure, a risk factor for heart disease and stroke. It can also cause fluid retention and kidney problems.

The recommended daily value (DV) for Sodium is 2,300g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Total Fat

Total fat is a macronutrient that provides energy and supports cell growth.

While necessary for the body, excessive total fat intake can contribute to obesity and related health issues.

The recommended daily value (DV) for Sodium is 78g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Protein

Protein is a macronutrient made up of amino acids that is essential for building and repairing tissues.

Protein is crucial for muscle development, immune function, and hormone production. Too little protein can lead to muscle weakness and fatigue, while too much may strain the kidneys.

The recommended intake is 50g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are the body's primary source of energy and are found in foods like bread, rice, pasta, and fruits.

Carbohydrates provide energy to the brain, muscles, and tissues.

Excessive intake can lead to weight gain, and increased risk of type 2 Diabetes.

The recommended daily value (DV) for carbohydrates is 275g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Added Sugars

Added sugars are sugars that are added to foods during processing or preparation (e.g., table sugar, syrups).

Added sugars contribute to excess calorie intake and can lead to obesity and heart disease.

The recommended daily value (DV) for added sugars is 50g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Total Sugars

Total sugars refer to both natural sugars, like those in fruits, and added sugars found in processed foods.

Excessive intake of sugar can contribute to weight gain and increase the risk of chronic diseases like diabetes.

The recommended daily value (DV) for sugar is 50g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Vitamin D

Adequate vitamin D is essential for bone health and immune system support.

Deficiency can lead to weakened bones and increase risk of infections

Saturated Fat

Saturated Fat is a type of fat primarily found in animal products and some plant oils.

Excessive intake raises cholesterol, increasing the risk of heart disease.

The recommended daily value (DV) for Saturated Fat is 20g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Dietary Fiber

Dietary fiber is a type of carbohydrate that the body cannot digest andit helps regulate the digestive system.

Fiber helps prevent constipation and supports overall gut health.

The recommended daily value (DV) for Dietary Fiber is 28g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Iron

Iron is a mineral essential for producing hemoglobin, which carries oxygen in the blood.

Iron is crucial for transporting oxygen throughout the body.

The recommended daily value (DV) for Iron is 28g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Calcium

Calcium is a mineral that is vital for bone health and muscle function.

Calcium is necessary for maintaining strong bones and preventing osteoporosis.

The recommended daily value (DV) for Calcium is 28g per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.

Potassium

Potassium is a mineral and electrolyte that helps balance fluids in your body and supports proper muscle and nerve function

Potassium is essential for maintaining healthy blood pressure and preventing muscle cramps.

The recommended daily value (DV) for potassium is 4,700mg per day based on a 2,000-calorie diet.
